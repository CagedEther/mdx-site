## How does the solar calculator work? Let me tell you! 

Our calculator combines your monthly electricity usage, roof characteristics, and location data to estimate the ideal system size for your home. It then applies local panel pricing, available incentives, and your utility's net metering policy to project your savings over 25 years.

## What information do I need to get started?

You'll need three things: your average monthly electricity bill (or kWh usage), your ZIP code, and a rough idea of your roof's orientation (which direction it faces). Everything else — irradiance, utility rates, incentives — we look up automatically.

## How accurate is the estimate?

Our estimates are typically within 10–15% of actual installer quotes for the system size and cost, and within 5% for annual production estimates. The main source of variance is shading from trees or neighboring structures, which requires an on-site assessment to measure precisely.

## Does the calculator account for battery storage?

Yes. You can add a battery storage system (like the Tesla Powerwall or Enphase IQ Battery) to your estimate. The calculator will factor in the additional cost, the self-consumption benefits, and any battery-specific incentives.

## What is net metering and do I qualify?

Net metering lets you sell excess solar power back to the grid, earning credits on your bill. Over 40 states require utilities to offer net metering, though the rates vary. Our calculator automatically checks whether your utility offers net metering and applies the correct credit rate.

## How long does a solar system actually last?

Most solar panels carry a 25-year performance warranty and typically last 30–35 years. Inverters usually need replacement around year 10–15, which we factor into our 25-year financial model. Panel output degrades roughly 0.5% per year — we model this accurately.

## When should I get an actual installer quote?

Use our calculator to determine whether solar makes financial sense for your situation. Once you're confident the economics work, get 3–4 quotes from certified installers in your area. Our estimates are designed to help you evaluate those quotes and spot outliers.
